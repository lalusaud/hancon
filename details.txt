About HaNCON The Hamilton Nepalese Community Organization & Network (HaNCON) is a not for profit organization established in 2023 to preserve Nepalese culture while empowering individuals and families across the Greater Hamilton Area to thrive in Canada’s multicultural community. Through responsive, community-driven programming, HaNCON delivers meaningful impact in cultural preservation, mental and physical well-being, intergenerational connection, and active civic participation.

Our Core Strategic Pillars:

Culture & Heritage: Celebrating Nepalese traditions, languages, and festivals.

Social Inclusion: Building supportive spaces for youth, women, and seniors to prevent isolation.

Capacity & Empowerment: Providing skill development, mentorship, and leadership opportunities.

Community Engagement: Partnering locally to foster diversity, equity, and collective impact.
